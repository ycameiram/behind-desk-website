The first 30 days

A structured path from orientation to ownership.

The exact pace can vary by role and client, but our onboarding approach is designed to create clarity early and build confidence over time.

WEEK 1
ORIENTATION & ACCESS

Learn the business. Understand the role. Get set up.

The first week is about building a strong foundation. Your remote professional becomes familiar with your business, team, systems, workflows, communication channels, and expectations. We help coordinate access and make sure the role is clearly understood before responsibilities begin to ramp up.

WEEK 2
RAMP-UP & EXECUTION

Start doing the work. Build confidence.

Your professional begins taking on core responsibilities with appropriate guidance and oversight. We help identify questions, clarify processes, and address gaps early so the transition from learning to execution is smoother.

WEEK 3
OWNERSHIP & PARTNERSHIP

Less hand-holding. More ownership.

As familiarity grows, your professional takes greater ownership of their responsibilities and becomes more integrated into your workflow. The goal is not simply to complete tasks, but to contribute meaningfully to your team.

WEEK 4 & BEYOND
OPTIMIZE & GROW

The onboarding ends. The partnership continues.

Our support doesn't stop once your professional is fully onboarded. We remain available to help with communication, performance, training, workflow improvements, and changing business needs.
